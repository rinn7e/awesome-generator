## Job Portals

Primary job boards and vacancy aggregators operating in Cambodia.

- [BongThom](https://www.bongthom.com) - Leading job portal in Cambodia specializing in corporate, NGO, and technical vacancies.
- [CamHR](https://www.camhr.com) - Popular job search engine and employment platform for commercial and corporate positions.
- [Pelheap](https://www.pelheap.com) - Online career platform matching job seekers with Cambodian recruitment opportunities.
- [JobNet Cambodia](https://www.jobnet.com.kh) - Job vacancy marketplace covering various professional industries across Cambodia.

## Government and Official Portals

Official public employment resources and government job placement services.

- [National Employment Agency (NEA)](https://www.nea.gov.kh) - Official Cambodian government portal under Ministry of Labour providing job listings and workforce services.

## Professional Networks

Global networks filtered for job listings in Cambodia.

- [LinkedIn Cambodia Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/jobs-in-cambodia) - Professional network job board filtering corporate and technical positions in Cambodia.
